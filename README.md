# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product quantity, monthly trends, and geographic sales distribution.

The project focuses on transforming raw sales data into an interactive and business-oriented dashboard that makes key performance indicators and sales patterns easier to understand.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project was to build an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard that provides a consolidated view of e-commerce sales performance.

The dashboard allows users to explore sales data from multiple perspectives, including:

- Revenue performance
- Quantity sold
- Average unit price
- Customer analysis
- Monthly sales trends
- Top spending customers
- Product/unit quantity performance
- Geographic sales distribution
- Top-performing cities
- Year-wise sales comparison

The report also includes interactive filtering, allowing users to analyze the dashboard across different years.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Revenue Analysis

The Revenue section provides an overview of total sales performance and year-over-year comparison.

It helps users monitor:

- Total revenue
- Revenue growth
- Revenue performance across periods
- Year-over-year changes

---

### 2. Quantity Analysis

The Quantity KPI provides an overview of the total quantity sold.

The accompanying trend visualization helps understand how sales volume changes over time.

---

### 3. Average Unit Price

The Average Unit Price KPI provides an overview of the average selling price across the available sales data.

This can be used alongside revenue and quantity metrics to understand overall sales performance.

---

### 4. Monthly Sales Trend

The Monthly Sales Trend visualization shows how total sales change throughout the year.

This allows users to identify:

- Monthly sales peaks
- Lower-performing months
- Sales fluctuations
- Potential seasonal patterns

---

### 5. Customer Analysis

The Customer Insight section provides a detailed view of customer sales performance.

The dashboard highlights top spending customers and provides additional information including:

- Customer name
- Total sales
- Order count
- Quantity purchased

This helps users explore customer-level sales performance.

---

### 6. Top 5 Units by Quantity Sold

The dashboard identifies the top five units based on quantity sold.

The displayed results include:

| Unit | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| pk | 228K |
| pack | 113K |
| tubs | 67K |

These results provide a quick view of the units contributing the highest sales volume.

### 7. Geographic Sales Distribution

The geographic section combines a map visualization with a city-level sales ranking.

The dashboard highlights sales distribution across cities including:

|---|---:|

| Chittagong | 20M |

### 8. Interactive Year Filter

The dashboard includes an interactive year slicer.

Users can select different years to dynamically update the dashboard and explore changes in:

- Revenue
- Quantity
- Customer performance
- Monthly sales
- Product/unit performance
- Geographic sales distribution
